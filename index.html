      How the forecast works

      Each of 5,000 simulated seasons plays out the remaining regular-season schedule week by week. A team's score in a week is drawn from a normal distribution whose mean blends its projected starting-lineup points for that week (weighted 75%) with its season scoring average (25%) (Sleeper's player projections run through this league's scoring), and whose spread is the team's own scoring volatility this season, pulled toward the league-wide volatility when the sample is small. Standings in every simulated season use win percentage, then points for; the top ${'${PO}'} seeds make the playoffs. Picks in the calculator do not change the forecast above.
